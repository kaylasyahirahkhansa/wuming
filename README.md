无名。
Wú míng.
Nameless.

无欲，万物皆明。
Wú yù, wànwù jiē míng.
Without desire, all is clear.

而欲无欲，一也。
Ér yù wú yù, yī yě.
Yet desire and its absence are one.

万物自运，无所为也。
Wànwù zì yùn, wú suǒ wéi yě.
All things run their own course. Nothing needs to be done.

虽为无成，因物自运。
Suī wéi wú chéng, yīn wù zì yùn.
Even though you act, nothing is accomplished. For things run on their own.

人我无别，彼此为一。
Rén wǒ wú bié, bǐ cǐ wéi yī.
There is no distinction between self and other, this and that are also one.

天地无别，虽二而一。
Tiān dì wú bié, suī èr ér yī.
Heaven and Earth are not different, though two, they are one.

彼此忘矣，如此而已。
Bǐ cǐ wàng yǐ, rú cǐ ér yǐ.
This and that have been forgotten, that is all.

复无不归，一。
Fù wú bù guī, yī.
Return to nothing and not returning is one.
